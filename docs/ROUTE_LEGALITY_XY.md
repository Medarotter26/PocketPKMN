# Walker courses for X / Y: are the Pokemon legal?

Generated by `tools/check_route_legality.py` from `romfs/routes/xy.txt` (108 course Pokemon on 18 courses) and PokeAPI's CSV tables in `art_src/pokeapi/` (github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi, data/v2/csv). Re-run the script after changing the courses.

Each Pokemon is checked twice, once as if it goes into an **X** save and once into a **Y** save, because the walker gives every course to both. The course species are kept as they are.

The met level is free: a Pokemon may have been met at any legal level at or below its course level and then grown. An evolved species can be generated as if its pre-evolution was caught wild and then evolved.

**Categories** (checked in this order)

- **WILD_OK** - wild in that game with a repeatable method (grass, horde, surf, rods, rock smash, flowers, rough terrain, ambushes, berry trees) starting at or below the course level. Shown: met level to use and where.
- **EVO_FROM_WILD** - not wild itself, but an earlier member of its line is (met at or below the course level), and every evolution step up to the course species is non-trade (level-up, item, happiness).
- **EVO_NEEDS_TRADE** - as EVO_FROM_WILD, but a step is a trade evolution: legal only with a traded-once history (another trainer as past handler).
- **EVO_LEVEL** - a pre-evolution is wild, but the course level is below the evolution level.
- **WILD_LEVEL** - wild (itself or a pre-evolution), but every encounter starts above the course level. The lowest wild level and place are shown.
- **STATIC** - only a one-time gift or static encounter in that game.
- **EGG_OK** - hatched from a breedable parent and evolved without trading; met as an egg at Lv 1. Used when no line member is wild, and (user 2026-11-15: "Yes, use eggs") whenever the wild / gift origin does not fit the course level.
- **EGG_NEEDS_TRADE** - as EGG_OK, but a step is a trade evolution: legal only with a traded-once history.
- **EGG_LEVEL** - as EGG_OK, but the course level is below the evolution level.
- **TRADE_EVO** - no line member wild and only reachable by a trade evolution.
- **NOT_OBTAINABLE** - cannot be caught or hatched in that game (legendary, Undiscovered egg group, Ditto, ...).

For WILD_LEVEL, EVO_LEVEL and STATIC the egg route is shown too. Moves were not checked.

## Data caveats

- PokeAPI's X/Y tables contain grass, horde, flowers, rough terrain, surf, rods, rock smash, ambush, berry-tree, gift and static encounters, and the Friend Safari (filed as walk at location Friend Safari, Lv 30, with a slot condition; shown here as method friend-safari and counted as a repeatable wild catch - it needs a friend's safari type and post-game access).
- Location area names are PokeAPI's; some X/Y areas only have a number ("unknown area NNN").
- PokeAPI's X/Y gift/static list is short (10 rows per game); a one-time encounter missing there shows as not wild.
- Evolution rows come from PokeAPI; rows tied to later games are ignored. Happiness and item evolutions are taken as possible from Lv 2 / any level.
- Moves, abilities, forms and Poke Ball were not checked.
